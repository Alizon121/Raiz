# Privacy Policy for Raiz

**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

This policy explains what information Raiz ("the app," "we," "us") collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. This document is a plain-language draft and has not been reviewed by an attorney — if you're relying on it for an App Store/Play Store submission, have it reviewed before publishing.

## Information We Collect

### Account information
When you sign in (email/password, Google, or Apple), we collect your **email address** and **display name**, and create an account record tied to your unique user ID. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only receive the private relay address Apple provides.

### Scan history
Each time you scan or manually look up a produce item, we store the crop identified, the PLU code entered, and the date/time in your account's scan history, so you can review past scans. This is retained until you request deletion (see **Your Choices** below).

### Subscription status
If you subscribe to remove ads, we store your subscription status. We do not receive or store your payment details — purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, per their own privacy policies.

### Device/advertising data (free tier only)
If you use the free, ad-supported version of the app, Google AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. On iOS, this only includes personalized ad data if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission when prompted — declining still lets you use the app normally, with non-personalized ads. See [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for how AdMob handles this data.

### What we don't collect
The camera is used only locally on your device to read PLU stickers — images are never uploaded or stored by us. Pesticide/residue data shown in the app comes from public USDA, EPA, and FDA datasets and isn't tied to your personal data in any way.

## How We Use Information

- To operate your account and sync your scan history across devices
- To determine whether ads should be shown (subscription status)
- To serve and measure ads, if you're on the free tier (via AdMob)
- We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for purposes beyond operating the app.

## Third-Party Services

Raiz uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Firebase** (Google) — authentication and data storage
- **Google AdMob** — ad serving (free tier only)
- **Apple App Store / Google Play Billing** — subscription purchases

## Your Choices

- You can decline App Tracking Transparency on iOS; ads will still show, just non-personalized.
- You can restore or cancel your subscription at any time through your App Store or Google Play account settings.
- To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

Raiz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
