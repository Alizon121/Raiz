Frequently asked

How do I scan produce?

Open the Scan tab and point your camera at the PLU sticker on the item. If a code doesn't scan cleanly, you can also enter the PLU number manually.

Where does the pesticide and residue data come from?

Raíz combines public data from the EPA Pesticide Product Label System, the USDA/FDA Pesticide Data Program, and USDA NASS Quick Stats. It reflects typical, statistical data for that crop type — not a lab test of the specific item you scanned.

I subscribed to Remove Ads, but I still see ads. What do I do?

Open Settings → Remove Ads and tap "Restore purchases." This re-checks your subscription with the App Store and should clear ads within a few seconds. If it still doesn't resolve, email us with the approximate time you subscribed and which device/platform you're on.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data (scan history, favorites) and can't be undone.

Something else isn't working — what should I include when I email you?

The more detail the better: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and your device model/OS version if it's a display or crash issue.
